Privacy policy

A clear explanation of data handling, the services involved and the choices you have.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Your app data

Work sessions, plans, goals, reminders and preferences are stored on your device. PaceQ has no account system and does not send these records to a developer-controlled server.

Apple purchases

Apple StoreKit processes and restores lifetime access. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store payment card details.

Local notifications

With your permission, reminders are scheduled on your device. Their content is not sent to a developer-controlled server.

Analytics and advertising

PaceQ does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

Your choices

Control notifications in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data. Lifetime access can be restored using the same Apple Account.

This website

This static site has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or contact forms. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and technical request information to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Contact and your choices

Voluntary support emails are processed to answer your request; app records are not attached automatically. You can email requests to access, correct or delete personal information in support messages. Messages are kept as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.
