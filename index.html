How does the seven-day trial work?

Every PaceQ feature is available for seven days after onboarding. After the trial, one lifetime purchase unlocks the app permanently. There is no subscription.

Can I correct a missed start time?

Yes. While a session is active, choose Correct start and enter the time you actually began. PaceQ replaces the timing instead of adding another session.

Where is my data stored?

Your sessions, plans, and reminders stay on your device. PaceQ does not require an account or send this information to a developer-controlled server.
