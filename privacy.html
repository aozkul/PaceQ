Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Data we handle

PaceQ stores work sessions, plans, goals, reminders, and app preferences on your device so the app can provide its features. PaceQ does not operate an account system and does not send this information to a developer-controlled server.

Purchases

PaceQ uses Apple's StoreKit service to process and restore the lifetime purchase. Apple processes payment information under its own privacy policy. PaceQ does not receive or store your payment card details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, PaceQ schedules local notifications on your device for reminders you create. Notification content is not sent to a developer-controlled server.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

PaceQ does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Your choices

You can remove local PaceQ data by deleting the app. You can control notification permission in iOS Settings. A lifetime purchase may be restored through the Restore purchases option when using the same Apple Account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.
